=====
Act as a Principal Systems Analyst and Senior Technical Writer. I need you to analyze the concept of "local-first software architecture" and break it down for a mixed audience of software engineers and product managers.

Please structure your response with the following mandatory constraints:

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: Write a 3-sentence high-level overview explaining what local-first software is and why it matters right now.
2. CORE PILLARS: Create a detailed comparison table with columns: [Pillar Name | Traditional Cloud-Centric Approach | Local-First Alternative | Primary Trade-off]. Include at least 4 distinct pillars (e.g., data ownership, latency, collaboration/sync, offline durability).
3. EDGE CASE SCENARIO: Describe a realistic conflict-resolution scenario where two users edit the exact same document offline simultaneously and reconnect later. Explain how CRDTs (Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types) or operational transformation handle this, written in plain English without overly dense academic jargon.
4. ACTIONABLE CHECKLIST: Provide a 5-item bulleted checklist that a product team must evaluate before deciding to migrate a standard SaaS app to a local-first architecture.
5. ANTI-PATTERN WARNING: Conclude with a single paragraph highlighting the biggest trap or misconception developers fall into when attempting local-first design.

Tone requirements: Authoritative, pragmatic, clear, and free of generic AI fluff words (e.g., "delve", "testament", "tapestry", "in today's digital landscape").
