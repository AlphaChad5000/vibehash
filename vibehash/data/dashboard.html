Recovery words

These 24 words restore your wallet if this computer is lost, and anyone who has them can take its coins. Write them down somewhere safe, offline. They are shown here once; the file they are in stays in your Vibe Hash folder until you delete it.

How it works

Mining is training. Every few minutes the network hands your machine a piece of a model to train; other miners check it, and the block pays you for what checks out.
